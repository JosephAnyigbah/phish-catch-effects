## Files in this folder

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `KNUST_Staff_Questionnaire.docx` | Respondent-ready questionnaire: introduction, five consent boxes, sections A to F. Build the online form from it, or print it |
| `knust_survey_items.csv` | The same questions as a table for KoboToolbox or Google Forms |
| `knust_codebook.csv` | Maps each export column to an item, its range and its reverse flag |
| `adaptation_log.md` | Every wording change, who approved it and why |
| `cvi_results.md` | Expert content-validity ratings |

Response formats: consent are required tick boxes, section A is single choice, the skip question is
yes/no, sections B and D are 7-point ratings labelled at the ends, sections C and F are 7-point
agreement scales labelled at every point, and section E is a 7-point bipolar scale. Keep these
formats. They match the open data.
