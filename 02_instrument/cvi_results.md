# Content validity (CVI)

Panel of 5 to 8 adults (for example a KNUST UITS security officer, CS lecturers, a psychologist).
Each rates every item for relevance on a 4-point scale. I-CVI = share of experts rating 3 or 4.
Convention: I-CVI of .78 or more, S-CVI/Ave of .90 or more (VERIFY source).
